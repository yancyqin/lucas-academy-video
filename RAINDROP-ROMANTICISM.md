# Pressure? Anxiety? Chopin? Romanticism?

The published narrated film by Yancy & Eunice: [watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-mjN--klfjM).
The final revision is `v11-smooth60`, stored locally under production folder `v12`.
It is a 16:26, 1920×1080, 60 fps film with English Louise narration, a published
Chinese Fangfang audio track, and separate English and Simplified Chinese subtitles.

The film moves from a real gallery into the shared Romanticism salon, follows
Chopin and artists responding to upheaval and nature, then visits mono no aware.
The museum is a continuous walk through the current hall, with painting holds,
full-image appreciation, original footage and approved Muse inserts.
