## Render stability

A long four-worker render encountered Chromium’s Target closed error near the browser-lab section. V2 therefore renders inclusive 3,000-frame ranges with two workers, 256 MiB OffthreadVideo cache and 128 MiB media cache, opening a fresh browser per range. Completed chunks are checked for exact frame count and dimensions, then cached with SHA256 receipts under a source/props fingerprint. FFmpeg concatenates the picture chunks before muxing the two full-length audio tracks. Only complete matching chunks are reused.

This follows Remotion’s [Target closed guidance](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/target-closed) and documented [frame range/cache options](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/renderer/render-media). It does not change the timeline, experimental speed animation or audio timing.
