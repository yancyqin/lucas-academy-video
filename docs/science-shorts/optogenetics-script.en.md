# How did light help him find the notebook?



**o01** A blind patient reached for a notebook. How did light help him find it?

**o02** The clue starts with tiny algae. Shine a light, and they swim toward it.

**o03** Scientists found a door that light could open. Put it in other cells, and light could trigger a signal.

**o04** This patient’s light-sensing cells had failed. But other cells that could pass signals along were still there.

**o05** The team gave those cells another kind of light switch, one that responds to amber light.

**o06** Special glasses turn changes in brightness into pulses of light. That is encoding.

**o07** The brain learns to read those signals as objects. That is decoding.

**o08** After months of training, he could locate some objects and reach out to touch them.

**o09** This was partial recovery in one patient. He still needed the glasses, and normal vision had not returned.

**o10** Starting with a light signal, we can explore how the brain processes information.
