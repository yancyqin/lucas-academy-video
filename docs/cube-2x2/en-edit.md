# How Do You Solve a 2×2 Cube? English narration (v0.1 · 2026-10-23 · for Yancy to approve)

Cube series, episode 1. By Lucas Academy Team (names to be confirmed by Yancy). Plan: [CUBE-2X2.md](../../CUBE-2X2.md).
The Chinese film uses the same footage and the same examples ([zh-edit.md](zh-edit.md)); the cue ids match one to one.

One line: **3.6 million scrambles, one little move and two algorithms, three steps.**

Reading vs captions (the voice's spellings go in SPOKEN):
- Notation: R′ is read "R prime", U2 "U two", B2 "B two", R2 "R two".

Overall tone: curious, unhurried, discovering together with a child (README default, louise/en).

## CB00 · Opening: only eight pieces

- cb00-01 | This is a two-by-two cube. It has only eight little pieces, but scrambled, it can end up in more than three and a half million different ways.
  Picture: the scrambled cube turns slowly; the number counts up to 3,674,160
- cb00-02 | Can every single one of them be solved? Yes. And all you need is one little move and two algorithms.
- cb00-03 | So let's try it together: <strong>how do you solve a two-by-two cube?</strong>
  Picture: title card

## CB01 · Meet the cube

- cb01-01 | First, let's meet it. A two-by-two has no centre pieces. All eight pieces are corners, and each one has three colours.
  Picture: the solved cube turns slowly, every side in view
- cb01-02 | The colours come in pairs: white is opposite yellow, green is opposite blue, and red is opposite orange.
- cb01-03 | Now a few letters. R is the right layer: looking at it from the right, turn it clockwise.
  Picture: R turns, the letter R lights up
- cb01-04 | Add a little mark, R′, and it turns back the other way. U is the top layer, and U′ turns it back.
- cb01-05 | A 2 after a letter means a half turn. And a small y doesn't turn just one layer: you hold the whole cube and turn all of it.

## CB02 · Step one: the white layer

- cb02-01 | Step one: finish the bottom layer, with all the white facing down and the side colours of the four corners matching.
  Picture: the stage bar lights "1 The white layer"; the camera looks from below
- cb02-02 | A two-by-two has no centres, so we choose the first corner ourselves. Pick a white corner and hold it at the bottom front right, white facing down.
- cb02-03 | Ours is white, green and orange. From now on, the front is green and the right is orange: this one corner decides the colour of every side.
- cb02-04 | The next corner is white, green and red. First, turn the whole cube so that its spot comes to the bottom front right.
- cb02-05 | Now, just one little move: <strong>R U R′ U′</strong>. If once isn't enough, do it again.
- cb02-06 | On the third time, it drops right in, white facing down!
- cb02-07 | The next one, white, red and blue, is up on top. Turn the top to put it right above its spot, then R U R′ U′ again. This one takes five times.
- cb02-08 | The last one, white, blue and orange, needs only one. The first layer is done!
- cb02-09 | A tip: if a white corner is stuck in the bottom in the wrong spot, turn it to the front right and do R U R′ once to lift it out first.

## CB03 · Step two: a yellow top

- cb03-01 | White is on the bottom, so yellow is on top. Step two: make the whole top yellow.
- cb03-02 | This step uses our first algorithm. It's called <strong>Sune</strong>: R U R′ U R U2 R′.
- cb03-03 | How do you hold the cube? If exactly one top corner shows yellow, turn the top so it sits at the front left.
- cb03-04 | If none do, or two do, turn the top until the front-left corner's yellow faces left. Then do Sune.
- cb03-05 | Here, none of the top is yellow. Turn the top so the front-left corner's yellow faces left, and do Sune once.
- cb03-06 | Now exactly one corner shows yellow, and it's already at the front left. One more Sune… and the whole top is yellow!

## CB04 · Step three: the last corners

- cb04-01 | Last step: swap the four top corners into their right places.
- cb04-02 | Look at the sides. Is there a side whose two top corners are the same colour? They look like a pair of <strong>headlights</strong>.
- cb04-03 | Turn the headlights to the back, and do the second algorithm: R′ F R′ B2 R F′ R′ B2 R2.
- cb04-04 | If no side has headlights, do the algorithm once from anywhere, and they'll appear.
- cb04-05 | Finally, turn the top to line it up with the bottom… and it's solved!

## CB05 · Once more

- cb05-01 | Once more, all the way through: step one, the white layer; step two, a yellow top; step three, the last corners.
- cb05-02 | One little move and two algorithms. You can also open this cube on the Lucas Academy website and let it teach you, one step at a time.
