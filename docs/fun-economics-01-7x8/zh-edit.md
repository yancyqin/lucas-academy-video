## HX00 · 开场：你会算 7 × 8 吗？

- hx00-01 | 7 乘 8 等于多少？你一定脱口而出：56。那换个问法：不背口诀，只用两只手，你会算吗？
  > What is 7 times 8? You probably said 56 right away. So here's a different question: without the times table, using just your two hands, can you work it out?

## HX01 · 用手指算

- hx01-01 | 伸出两只手试试。7 比 5 多 2，左手伸出 2 根；8 比 5 多 3，右手伸出 3 根。
  > Hold up both hands and try it. 7 is 2 more than 5, so your left hand puts up 2 fingers; 8 is 3 more than 5, so your right hand puts up 3.
- hx01-02 | 伸出的手指一共 5 根，一根算一个十，就是 50。弯着的手指，左手 3 根，右手 2 根，3 乘 2 是 6。50 加 6，就是 56。
  > That's 5 fingers up, and each one counts as a ten: 50. The bent fingers: 3 on the left hand, 2 on the right. 3 times 2 is 6. 50 plus 6 is 56.

## HX02 · 为什么可以这样算

- hx02-01 | 为什么可以这样算？看弯着的手指。7 是 5 加 2，也是 10 减 3：左手弯着的 3 根，就是 7 离 10 还差的。右手弯着 2 根：8 是 10 减 2。
  > Why does this work? Look at the bent fingers. 7 is 5 plus 2, and it's also 10 minus 3: the 3 bent fingers on your left hand are how far 7 is from 10. Your right hand has 2 bent: 8 is 10 minus 2.
- hx02-02 | 所以 7 乘 8，就是 10 减 3，乘以 10 减 2。拆开来乘：100，减 30，减 20，再加 3 乘 2。
  > So 7 times 8 is 10 minus 3, times 10 minus 2. Split it up and multiply each part: 100, minus 30, minus 20, plus 3 times 2.
- hx02-03 | 100 是 10 个十，就像两只手的 10 根手指，一根一个十。减 30、减 20，就是去掉弯着的 3 根和 2 根，剩下伸出的 5 根，就是 50。最后加上 3 乘 2，正是弯着的手指相乘。
  > 100 is ten tens, like the 10 fingers on your two hands, one ten each. Minus 30 and minus 20 take away the 3 and the 2 bent fingers, which leaves the 5 fingers that are up: 50. And the 3 times 2 we add at the end is just the bent fingers multiplied.
- hx02-04 | 为什么最后要加上 3 乘 2？把 100 个点，排成 10 行、每行 10 个：减 30，划掉 3 列；减 20，划掉 2 行。右下角那 3 乘 2 个点，被划掉了两次，所以要加回来一次。剩下的，正好是 8 行、每行 7 个：56。
  > Why do we add 3 times 2 at the end? Draw 100 as 10 rows of 10 dots. Minus 30 crosses out 3 columns; minus 20 crosses out 2 rows. The corner, 3 times 2, got crossed out twice, so we add it back once. What's left is exactly 8 rows of 7: 56.
- hx02-05 | 6 到 10 的任意两个数，都是这样。弯着 x 根和 y 根：10 减 x，乘以 10 减 y，拆开来，是 10 减 x 减 y 这么多个十，再加 x 乘 y。10 根手指去掉弯着的，正是伸出的。
  > It works for any two numbers from 6 to 10. Say x fingers and y fingers are bent: 10 minus x, times 10 minus y, split up, gives this many tens: 10 minus x minus y; plus x times y. 10 fingers without the bent ones are exactly the fingers that are up.

## HX03 · 结尾

- hx03-01 | 它练的是：从 10 往回看。这就是思考的过程：一串复杂的步骤，每一步用的，都是简单的知识。这个过程，很宝贵。
  > What it trains: counting back from 10. And this is what thinking looks like: a long chain of steps, and every step uses only simple things you already know. That process is precious.
- hx03-02 | 换成 6 乘 7 试试：伸出几根，弯着几根？算出来的，和口诀一样吗？
  > Now try 6 times 7: how many fingers are up, and how many are bent? Does your answer match the times table?
