# 都学了三年，放弃就白学了吗？中文配音稿（v1.0 · 2026-10-18）

教育经济学 2（Education Economics 2）：沉没成本。作者 Yancy Qin | Lucas Academy。方案见 [FUN-ECONOMICS-02.md](../../FUN-ECONOMICS-02.md)。

一句话主线：**过去的，已经沉下去了；将来，还没有来；能选的，只有现在。** 沉没成本教我们往前看。

Yancy 定的（2026-10-16～18）：
- 直接讲 Mary 学钢琴三年：从有兴趣到没兴趣，遇到困难想放弃。家长多半不同意，因为花了钱和时间。
- 坚持是好的，但如果理由错了，就是沉没成本谬误。**先**讲往前看的更好理由（练耐心和韧劲、新的机会、停一停重新想），**再**讲沉没成本谬误。
- 例子：乐高为主（舍不得拆，总想怎么修），加第一步错了、越算越复杂的数学作业（例子是编的，接第一集的 7 × 8）。例子讲付出的工夫，不讲钱（美国孩子不太在乎钱）。
- 彩蛋：腓立比书（3:7、3:13–14，和合本）。
- 中英文各一部片（10-19），同一份分镜；画面上不放文字标签，片头片尾用这部片的语言。每部片都带中英两份字幕。

写法同第一集：每句下面 `>` 开头的是英文翻译（英文字幕和英文配音稿），不读。数字配音时写成汉字。

整体语气: 请用好奇、慢慢探索的语气，像带着孩子一起发现一样说。
整体语速: 1.0


## SC00 · 学了三年，不想学了

- sc00-01 | Mary 学钢琴，已经三年了。刚开始，她每天都想去碰一碰琴键；弹会第一首小曲子那天，她开心了一整个晚上。
  > Mary has been learning piano for three years. At first, she wanted to touch the keys every day; the day she learned her first little piece, she was happy all evening.
- sc00-02 | 后来，曲子越来越难，一首要练好几个星期；每天的练习，也越来越像一件不得不做的事。
  > Then the pieces got harder, each one taking weeks to learn, and practice began to feel more and more like a chore.
- sc00-03 | Mary：我不想学钢琴了。
  > I don't want to do piano anymore.
- sc00-04 | 如果你是 Mary 的爸爸妈妈，你会怎么回答？
  > If you were Mary's mom or dad, what would you say?

## SC01 · 「都学了三年了」

- sc01-01 | 很多爸爸妈妈的第一反应是：都学了三年了，学费交了那么多，时间也花了那么多，现在放弃，不就白学了？应该坚持。
  > Many parents' first reaction is: it's been three years, we've paid so much for lessons and spent so much time; if you quit now, wasn't it all for nothing? You should stick with it.
- sc01-02 | 坚持，当然是好的。只是坚持，也要看是因为什么。
  > Sticking with things is good, of course. But it matters why we stick with them.

## SC02 · 往前看，有更好的理由

- sc02-01 | 往前看，这时候其实有很多值得想的事。
  > Looking forward, there's actually a lot worth thinking about at a moment like this.
- sc02-02 | 第一，眼前这一段难，正是练耐心、练韧劲的时候。熬过去，Mary 会多一样本领：知道难的时候，自己也撑得下来。
  > First, a hard stretch like this is exactly when patience and grit get practiced. If she gets through it, Mary gains something new: knowing she can hold on when things are hard.
- sc02-03 | 第二，这也许是一个新的机会：Mary 喜欢的，到底是什么？是弹琴，是听音乐，还是想自己写一段旋律？换一首喜欢的曲子，换一种学法，路也许就不一样了。
  > Second, this may be a new opportunity: what is it Mary really loves? Playing? Listening to music? Or writing a melody of her own? With a piece she loves, or a different way of learning, the path may look different.
- sc02-04 | 第三，停一停，也是一个机会。放松下来，重新想一想，不等于放弃。
  > Third, a pause can be an opportunity too. Relaxing and thinking it over again is not the same as giving up.
- sc02-05 | 这些理由都在前面，都比「钱都交了，不能白费」强。
  > All these reasons look forward, and every one of them is stronger than "we've paid for it, it cannot go to waste."

## SC03 · 沉没成本

- sc03-01 | 那，「钱都交了、学了三年，不能白费」，问题出在哪儿？
  > So what's wrong with "we've paid for it, it's been three years, it cannot go to waste"?
- sc03-02 | 经济学里，已经花掉、而且不管接下来怎么选都拿不回来的，叫<strong>沉没成本</strong>，就像沉到水底的船。
  > In economics, what's already spent and cannot be gotten back, whatever you choose next, is called a sunk cost, like a ship that has sunk to the bottom of the sea.
- sc03-03 | 三年的学费和时间：坚持学，拿不回来；不学了，也拿不回来。它在两条路上一样重，像天平两边放了一样重的砝码，天平不会因为它动一下。所以，它帮不了我们选。
  > Three years of fees and time: keep learning, and you don't get them back; stop, and you don't get them back either. They weigh the same on both paths, like equal weights on both sides of a scale; the scale will not move an inch because of them. So they cannot help us choose.
- sc03-04 | 明明帮不了选，却还让它来做决定，经济学家管这个叫<strong>沉没成本谬误</strong>。
  > When something cannot help us choose, but we let it decide anyway, economists call it the sunk cost fallacy.

## SC04 · 拼错的乐高

- sc04-01 | 这个坑，小孩子也常掉进去。乐高拼到一半，发现前面有一块放错了。
  > Kids fall into this trap too. Halfway through a LEGO build, you find a brick in the wrong place, several steps back.
- sc04-02 | 拆掉重拼？舍不得，这可是拼了一下午的。于是想办法修：这里加一块，那里绕一下，越修越难修。
  > Take it apart and rebuild? No way, that's a whole afternoon of building. So you try to patch it: a brick here, a detour there, and the more you patch, the harder it gets.
- sc04-03 | 往前看，该问的只有一句：从现在到拼好，哪条路最快？有时候，拆掉几步，反而最快。
  > Looking forward, there's only one question: from here to the finished model, which way is fastest? Sometimes taking a few steps apart is the fastest way.
- sc04-04 | 而且拆了，积木一块也没少；第二次拼，还会快很多，因为你已经知道怎么拼了。
  > And when you take it apart, you don't lose a single brick; the second build goes much faster, because now you know how.

## SC05 · 第一步错了的作业

- sc05-01 | 写作业也一样。Mary 的数学作业：一盒铅笔有 8 支，7 盒平均分给 14 个同学，每人分到几支？
  > Homework is the same. Mary's math homework: a box holds 8 pencils, and 7 boxes are shared equally among 14 classmates. How many pencils does each one get?
- sc05-02 | 她第一步写成了 7 加 8，等于 15。15 支分给 14 个人，每人一支，还剩一支。难道要把它切成 14 段？
  > Her first step was 7 plus 8, which makes 15. 15 pencils for 14 kids: one each, with one left over. Should she cut it into 14 pieces?
- sc05-03 | Mary 舍不得擦，接着算，越算越复杂，算出来的数，早就不像这份作业该有的样子了。
  > Mary doesn't want to erase it, so she keeps going; the more she works, the messier it gets, and the numbers stop looking like anything this homework should have.
- sc05-04 | 这时候最该做的，是停下来，回到第一步：7 盒，每盒 8 支，是 7 乘 8，56 支。56 支分给 14 个人，每人 4 支。
  > What she should do is stop and go back to step one: 7 boxes of 8 pencils is 7 times 8, 56 pencils. 56 pencils for 14 kids: 4 each.
- sc05-05 | 不过，数学有时候不一样：有的题本来就要算很久，接着算才是对的。怎么分辨？还是看前面：数字是不是越来越怪？答案说得通吗？接着算和重新开始，哪个更快？
  > Math can be different, though: some problems really do take a long time, and keeping going is right. How can you tell? Look forward again: are the numbers getting strange? Does the answer make sense? Which is faster, keeping going or starting over?
- sc05-06 | 算错的那几步，也没有白算：下一次，Mary 会先把第一步想清楚。
  > And the wrong steps weren't wasted either: next time, Mary will think the first step through.

## SC06 · 为什么舍不得

- sc06-01 | 拼好的乐高、写了一半的作业，为什么这么舍不得？
  > A half-built LEGO model, half-finished homework: why are they so hard to let go of?
- sc06-02 | 有研究发现，人对自己亲手做的东西，会看得特别重；看着自己拼好的乐高被人当面拆掉，连接着拼的劲都会少很多。
  > Research has found that people value what they make with their own hands especially highly; and people who watch their finished LEGO models taken apart in front of them lose much of their drive to keep building.
- sc06-03 | 还有研究发现，在这件事上，大人比小孩子更容易犯错：大人太熟悉「不能浪费」这条规矩了，连用不上的地方也用上了。
  > Other research has found that here, adults slip up more easily than young children: adults know the rule "don't waste" so well that they use it even where it doesn't apply.
- sc06-04 | 不能浪费，当然是好规矩。只是它管的，是还没花出去的钱和时间；已经沉下去的，它管不了。
  > "Don't waste" is a good rule, of course. But it's about the money and time we haven't spent yet; it has no say over what has already sunk.

## SC07 · 回到钢琴：现在的选择

- sc07-01 | 回到 Mary。三年的学费和时间沉下去了；可三年学会的东西，一样也没沉：识谱，乐感，手指，还有听音乐的耳朵，都在她身上。所以不管怎么选，都不是白学。
  > Back to Mary. Three years of fees and time have sunk; but what she learned in those three years hasn't sunk at all: reading music, her musical feel, her fingers, and her ear for music are all still with her. So whatever she chooses, it wasn't for nothing.
- sc07-02 | 也要小心另一个方向：今天特别累、特别烦，也会让人想放弃。今天的心情，不等于前面的路。
  > Watch out for the other direction too: a day when you're especially tired or fed up can make you want to quit as well. Today's mood is not the path ahead.
- sc07-03 | 所以该问的，只有前面：带着已经学会的这些，接下来这一段路，还想走吗？用什么方式走？
  > So the only question is about what's ahead: with everything she has already learned, does she still want to walk this path? And how?
- sc07-04 | 过去的，已经沉下去了；将来，还没有来；能选的，只有现在。珍惜当下，就是认真做好现在这个选择。
  > The past has already sunk; the future hasn't come yet; all we can choose is now. Making the most of the present means making today's choice with care.

## SC08 · 彩蛋：保罗

- sc08-01 | 说起来，两千年前有一个人，身后「已经付出的」比谁都多。他叫保罗：出身、学问、在人眼里的好名声，样样都有。
  > Two thousand years ago, there was a man with more "already invested" behind him than almost anyone. His name was Paul: his family line, his learning, his good name in people's eyes; he had it all.
- sc08-02 | 可他在一封信里写：「只是我先前以为与我有益的，我现在因基督都当作有损的。」
  > Yet in one letter he wrote: "I consider those things that were gain to me as a loss for Christ."
- sc08-03 | 他还写：「我只有一件事，就是忘记背后，努力面前的，向着标竿直跑。」
  > He also wrote: "One thing I do: forgetting the things which are behind and stretching forward to the things which are before, I press on toward the goal."
- sc08-04 | 忘记背后，不是说过去不重要；是不让过去替你决定，下一步往哪里走。
  > Forgetting what is behind doesn't mean the past doesn't matter; it means not letting the past decide where you go next.

## SC09 · 结尾的问题

- sc09-01 | 那么，下一次，当孩子说「我不想学了」，或者你想劝自己再坚持一下的时候，你会问问自己：让你留下来的，是前面的路，还是身后已经走过的路？
  > So next time a child says "I don't want to do this anymore," or you want to talk yourself into hanging on a little longer, ask yourself: is it the path ahead that keeps you there, or the path you've already walked?
