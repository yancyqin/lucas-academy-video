## SC00 · 片头

- sc00-01 | 十组蝴蝶。每组里，一只是真的，一只是 AI 画的。
  > Ten pairs of butterflies. In each pair, one is real and one was drawn by AI.
- sc00-02 | 每组五秒，你来猜：哪只是真的？
  > Five seconds each. Which one is real?
- sc00-04 | 第一组。
  > Here is pair one.

## SC01 · 玻璃翼蝶

- sc01-01 | 这是玻璃翼蝶。它的翅膀上几乎没有鳞片，还有一层特别细的小柱子，几乎不反光，所以透明得像玻璃。
  > This is the glasswing. Its wings have almost no scales, plus a layer of tiny pillars that hardly reflect light, so they are clear as glass.
- sc01-02 | 它住在中美洲的雨林里，很常见。
  > It lives in the rainforests of Central America, and it is common.

## SC02 · 蓝摩门凤蝶

- sc02-01 | 【公布】最厉害的 AI 大模型，也都被骗了。Fable 还说，真的这只翅膀中间有一条奇怪的花纹，不像真蝴蝶。
  > Even the best AI models were fooled. Fable said the real one has a strange pattern across its wings, not like a real butterfly.
- sc02-02 | 这是蓝摩门凤蝶，只住在印度南部和斯里兰卡，在那儿很常见。
  > This is the blue Mormon. It lives only in southern India and Sri Lanka, where it is common.

## SC03 · 孔雀石蝶

- sc03-01 | 这是孔雀石蝶，名字来自一种绿色的石头，孔雀石。
  > This is the malachite, named after a green stone, malachite.
- sc03-02 | 它从巴西一直往北，住到美国佛罗里达的南部，在热带很常见。
  > It lives from Brazil all the way north to southern Florida, and it is common in the tropics.

## SC04 · 豹纹蛱蝶

- sc04-01 | 【公布】又是两个都错。AI 这张每个细节都清清楚楚，Fable 反倒嫌真的这只身子糊成一团。
  > Both wrong again. Every detail of the AI one is crisp, and Fable even said the real one's body was a smeared blob.
- sc04-02 | 这是一种豹纹蛱蝶，翅膀上的斑点像豹子。这一类蝴蝶种类很多，大多很常见。
  > This is a leopard butterfly, with spots like a leopard. There are many kinds, and most are common.

## SC05 · 孔雀蛱蝶

- sc05-01 | 这是孔雀蛱蝶，翅膀上有四只大眼睛。它从欧洲一直住到日本。
  > This is the European peacock, with four big eyes on its wings. It lives from Europe all the way to Japan.
- sc05-02 | 它是熬过冬天的蝴蝶，所以春天一暖和，最早飞出来的常常就是它。很常见。
  > It lives through the winter as an adult, so when spring warms up, it is often the first butterfly out. It is common.

## SC06 · 眼灰蝶

- sc06-01 | 这是一种眼灰蝶。合着翅膀是灰色的，雄蝶打开翅膀，才是亮蓝色。欧亚大陆的草地上很常见。
  > This is a blue. With its wings closed it looks grey; only when a male opens them do you see the bright blue. It is common in meadows across Eurasia.

## SC07 · 红斑紫蛱蝶

- sc07-01 | 【公布】这次只有 Fable 上当了。
  > This time, only Fable was fooled.
- sc07-02 | 这是红斑紫蛱蝶，住在美国东部的树林里。
  > This is the red-spotted purple. It lives in the woods of the eastern United States.
- sc07-03 | 它自己没有毒，可长得很像一种有毒的凤蝶，鸟一看，就不敢吃它。很常见。
  > It is not poisonous itself, but it looks a lot like a poisonous swallowtail, so birds leave it alone. It is common.

## SC08 · 美洲黑凤蝶

- sc08-01 | 这是美洲黑凤蝶，北美到处都有，还是俄克拉何马州的州蝶。
  > This is the black swallowtail. It is found all over North America, and it is Oklahoma's state butterfly.
- sc08-02 | 它的毛毛虫，爱吃胡萝卜和莳萝的叶子。
  > Its caterpillars love carrot and dill leaves.

## SC09 · 红带袖蝶

- sc09-01 | 【公布】Fable 被这只橙黄豆粉蝶骗了：后翅的「8」字斑、粉红色的翅边，AI 都画对了。
  > Fable was fooled by this clouded yellow: the figure-eight spot on the hindwing, the pink wing edges, the AI got them all right.
- sc09-02 | 这是红带袖蝶，住在中美洲和南美的森林里。
  > This is the postman. It lives in the forests of Central and South America.
- sc09-03 | 它会吃花粉，所以能活好几个月，比大多数蝴蝶长得多。很常见。
  > It eats pollen, so it can live for several months, much longer than most butterflies. It is common.

## SC10 · 鸟翼凤蝶

- sc10-01 | 这是鸟翼凤蝶，世界上最大的蝴蝶，就在这一类里。
  > This is a birdwing. The biggest butterfly in the world belongs to this group.
- sc10-02 | 它住在澳大利亚北部和新几内亚的雨林。国际上买卖它们，要有许可证。
  > It lives in the rainforests of northern Australia and New Guinea. Trading them across borders needs a permit.
