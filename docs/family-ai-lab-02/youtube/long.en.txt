【标题 / Title】
Can 0 and 1 Talk?

【简介 / Description】
Mia's paper note will not fit through the pipe. She and Leo try a shared code: gold for one, blue for zero.

Send PLAY, follow yes-or-no clues to a hidden gift, and find the watermelon snack. Then try a message of your own with the browser lab or a printed code table.

A story for families about binary messages, bits, bytes and asking useful questions.
