【标题 / Title】
Can 0 and 1 Talk?

【简介 / Description】
The note gets stuck. Tiny signals get the invitation through!

Join Mia and Leo for an adventure in space: send a message, find a hidden gift and send a reply. Their tiny pipe leads us to the internet, where videos, messages and even chats with AI use zeros and ones.

A funny story? A new game? What will you share today?

Try the browser lab and printable worksheets: https://ai.lucasacademy.org/courses/zero-and-one/

By Lucas Academy Team
Narration: Louise, synthetic voice in English and Chinese.
Fictional space scenes: Muse. Environment artwork: ImageGen. Teaching graphics, original music and sound effects: Lucas Academy.
