【标题 / Title】
Hope and Freedom

【简介 / Description】
A 58-second walk through the Romantic Salon, approaching two related paintings. See each whole work, then move closer to selected details.

From Géricault’s raft to Delacroix’s barricade: a raised cloth seeks rescue; a raised flag gathers a crowd. Two original paintings connect hope, collective struggle and freedom.
