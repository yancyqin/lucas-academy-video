【标题 / Title】
Silence and Time

【简介 / Description】
A 58-second walk through the Romantic Salon, approaching two related paintings. See each whole work, then move closer to selected details.

Friedrich pairs the quiet of an open sea with bare trees and a ruined abbey: two landscapes through which to consider silence and time.
