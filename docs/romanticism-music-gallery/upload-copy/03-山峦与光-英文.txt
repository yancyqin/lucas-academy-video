【标题 / Title】
Mountains and Light

【简介 / Description】
A 58-second walk through the Romantic Salon, approaching two related paintings. See each whole work, then move closer to selected details.

First take in the expansive mountain landscapes of Church and Bierstadt, then move closer to their waterfalls: water descending into a river and a lake.

English artwork and artist labels. Separate Simplified Chinese SRT. Instrumental music; no narration.
