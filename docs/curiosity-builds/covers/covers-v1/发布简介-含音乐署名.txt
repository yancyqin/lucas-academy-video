What Can Curiosity Build? | 9 Projects to Explore

A chess game with comeback rules. A ride built from formulas. A gallery made from our art.

This short film follows nine Lucas Academy projects from a simple idea, through the building process, into something we can play, explore, or create with—from art and living skies to puzzles, coding competitions, and Bible games.
